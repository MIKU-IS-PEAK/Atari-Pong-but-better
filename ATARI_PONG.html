Disconnected

Game Instructions

Controls

  • Player 1 (Left): W (up) / S (down) to move paddle
  • Player 2 (Right): ↑ (up) / ↓ (down) to move paddle
  • Player 1 Slow Ability: Press A to activate
  • Player 1 Speed Boost: Press D to activate
  • Player 1 Redirect: Press E to activate
  • Player 2 Slow Ability: Press ← (Left Arrow) to activate
  • Player 2 Speed Boost: Press → (Right Arrow) to activate
  • Player 2 Redirect: Press Shift to activate

Game Modes

  • Standard Mode: First player to reach 10 points wins
  • Timer Mode: Play for 1 minute, highest score wins
  • AI Mode: Play against AI with 3 difficulty levels

Abilities

  • Slow Ability (A / Left Arrow): Slows ball to 60% speed when it enters your side. Blue overlay shows when active. 5 second duration, 15 second cooldown.
  • Speed Boost Ability (D / Right Arrow): Any ball you hit speeds up to 175% for opponent. Paddle glows neon red when active. Speed boost is removed immediately when ball is hit or scores. 5 second duration, 15 second cooldown.
  • Redirect Ability (E / Shift): When activated while ball is going towards opponent, ball flashes black for 0.5 seconds and randomly launches in a different angle (not towards activator). When activated while ball is towards you, launches ball to the other side. 20 second cooldown. Plays ability activation sound.
  • Special Interactions: If both players hit ball with abilities active, speed boost is removed when outgoing. Opponent hitting sped-up ball removes the speed boost immediately.
  • Note: AI can use all abilities automatically when available.

AI Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: Ball at 70% speed, AI at 60% speed
  • Normal: Standard speeds, AI at 80% speed
  • Hard: Ball at 125% speed, AI at 100% speed
